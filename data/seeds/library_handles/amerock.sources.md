# Amerock — library_handles sources

## Access caveat (read first)
amerock.com returns HTTP 403 to every automated request, and old.amerock.com refuses
connections. Amerock pages were read only through web-search extracts of amerock.com /
old.amerock.com product pages. Several SKU codes were confirmed by distributor listings
(titles/URLs). Spot-check before committing.

Coverage is Amerock's **Bar Pulls** collection (their core contemporary/commercial bar pull,
carbon steel, sold in 10/20/25-packs to trade) plus its matching **Bar Pulls knob (BP19009)**.

Finish suffixes are taken from Amerock's own product titles and URLs: 26 Polished Chrome, G10
Satin Nickel, MB Matte Black, PN Polished Nickel, ORB Oil-Rubbed Bronze, BBR Black Bronze, BBZ
Golden Champagne (per amerock.com URL `golden-champagne_..._bp40517bbz`), GM Gunmetal, CBZ
Caramel Bronze, SS Stainless Steel.

## Specs (whole collection)
- C-c 76 / 96 / 128 / 192 / 256 mm. Projection 1-3/8 in (35 mm) on every size, as Amerock
  publishes it, e.g.
  https://www.amerock.com/Products/Detail/pid/883/s/128mm-BAR-PULLS-2000806
  https://www.amerock.com/Products/Detail/pid/751/s/192mm-BAR-PULLS-2000807
  https://www.amerock.com/Products/Detail/pid/669/s/OVERSIZED-256mm-BAR-PULLS-2000808
  https://old.amerock.com/Products/Detail/pid/4808 (BP4051626, 96 mm, 35 mm projection)
- Material: Amerock describes the collection as carbon steel. For the **SS** finish, the
  amerock.com 128 mm Stainless Steel page (old.amerock.com pid 754) says "Stainless Steel"
  while retailer copy says carbon steel. Because of that conflict, material is empty on the SS rows.
- Knob BP19009: 1-15/16 in (49 mm) long, projection 1-3/8 in (35 mm), single fixing, carbon steel.
  https://www.amerock.com/Products/Detail/pid/4804 and https://www.amerock.com/bar-pulls-cabinet-knob-bar--pulls--knob
