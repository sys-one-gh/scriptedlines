# Panolam / Nevamar — library_melamine sources

**No CSV written.** Not enough could be verified to build any decor x substrate x
thickness rows.

## What was tried

- panolam.com (product pages and every wp-content PDF, including the Nevamar/Pionite
  Product Availability Guides of 3/2026, 11/2025, 7/2025) returns a Sucuri JavaScript
  challenge to curl and HTTP 403 to WebFetch. The challenge was not bypassed.
- Wayback Machine: archived Nevamar PAG 1/2024 opened fine, but it covers **HPL
  only** (no TFL section). The March 2026 / 2025 PAGs and the TFL product pages are not
  archived.
- Panolam TFL brochure (Jan 2018, Panolam-authored, hosted by Craftsmen Hardwoods:
  https://craftsmenhardwoods.com/usercontent/documents/information/plywood/panolam%20tfl%20brochure.pdf)
  — 8 years old, the text layer is damaged (characters missing from many names), and
  it does not map designs to finish, substrate or thickness. It gives only general
  specs: PB or MDF, CARB P2 at minimum, ZCore NAUF on request, "All colors and designs
  are available as FSC certified", widths 49"/61", finishes (Cathedral, Chamois,
  Crystal, High Gloss, Polished Cathedral, Satin, Super Matte, Textural, Timberline,
  Natural Grain, Medium Gloss, Wood Essence).
- Distributor pages (Chesapeake Plywood, Rugby/ishoprugby) are JS-rendered, contain
  image-only PDFs, or list only distributor stock — not acceptable as the sole source
  for which decors exist.
