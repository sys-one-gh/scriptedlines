# Hafele — library_handles sources

## Access caveat (read first)
hafele.com / hafele.ca return HTTP 403 to automated requests, including the Intershop print PDFs.
Codes, finishes, c-c and material therefore come from **HomeDecorHardware.com's "The Complete
Hafele" item pages**, which reproduce Häfele America's item descriptions verbatim. One page per
item: https://www.homedecorhardware.com/hf-155-99-0NN.html, hf-155-00-78N.html, hf-155-01-28N.html.
Projection comes from Häfele's "length x height mm" dimension strings as reproduced in
**DK Hardware** listing titles, e.g. "155.99.002 Bar Handle, Steel ... 96 ... 136 x 35 mm". In
Häfele's handle drawings the height dimension (B) is the projection; DK's 155.00.992 title spells
this out as "dim. B: 35 mm". Several items were also confirmed via web-search extracts of
hafele.com/us product pages (P-00852598 Cosmopolitan, P-00854355 Elemental, P-00853335 Voyage).

`code` is Häfele's item number in its published dotted form (e.g. `155.99.016`).

## Rows
- **Cosmopolitan bar pull** (steel, 12 mm bar, M4 except 155.99.013 which is 8-32), projection 35 mm:
  155.99.000–155.99.017; 155.99.040 and 155.99.043 (320 mm); 155.02.411 and 155.02.415 (matte gold 96 / 192);
  155.00.721 (3 1/2 in = 88.9 mm) and 155.00.801 (4 in = 101.6 mm).
  The 35 mm height was seen on 155.99.002, .006, .017, .040, .043, 155.02.411, .415, 155.00.721 and .801,
  covering every size in the file. It is applied across the model.
  Some retailers (myknobs) call this family "Cornerstone"; Häfele's item text says Cosmopolitan.
- **Crescent arch handle** (steel), projection 30 mm (96: 112 x 30, 128: 148 x 30, per DK titles for .019/.021/.026/.027):
  155.99.018–155.99.027 and 155.99.032 (matte gold 96).
- **Chanterelle knob** (zinc alloy), height 28 mm and diameter 30 mm (DK title for 155.99.029):
  155.99.028–155.99.031.
- **Elemental bar pull** (steel, "stainless steel look" finish), projection 35 mm (156 mm item: "226 x 35 mm"; hafele.com
  P-00854355 extract gives 1 3/8 in): 155.00.780 (96), 155.00.781 (128), 155.00.783 (156 mm, as Häfele publishes it).
- **Voyage wire pull** (stainless steel 304, matte): 155.01.280 (160) and 155.01.281 (192) at 40 mm projection
  ("176 x 40" / "208 x 40", DK and poshhaus); 155.01.283 (288) and 155.01.284 (352) with projection empty (not seen).

## Finish names
Häfele's wording is normalized: "Matt chrome" → Matte Chrome, "Matt black" / "Black matt" → Matte Black,
"Matt gold" → Matte Gold, and the typo "Polished chorme" on 155.99.023 → Polished Chrome.

## Left out on purpose
- Items 155.99.033–035 and 155.00.778/779/782/784/785 and 155.01.276–279/282 had no item page (they redirect to an unrelated item).
- Häfele's larger US handle families (Bar Pull Radius P-01496396, Hollow SS P-01496358, Essex, Veranda, Tempes, Delaney, Futura,
  Architectural, Studio): their pages were seen, but item numbers could not be read because hafele.com blocks access.
- 117.64.616 (Crescent, matt stainless) was seen only once, so it was skipped.
