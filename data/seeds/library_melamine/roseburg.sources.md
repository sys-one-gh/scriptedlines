# Roseburg — library_melamine sources

Researched 2026-11-26. **No current Roseburg TFL collection exists.** All 24 pre-existing rows are kept unchanged but set to `is_active=0`. No new rows were added.

## Evidence
- Roseburg press release, March 20, 2024: the Missoula, MT particleboard plant closes May 22, 2024, "the final step in the company's strategic plan to exit the particleboard manufacturing business and focus resources on other product segments, including MDF, engineered wood, plywood, and lumber." https://www.roseburg.com/news-corporate/roseburg-to-end-operations-at-missoula-mt-particleboard-plant-permanently-close-facility/
- Roseburg's own page says Duramine was one of the brands made in Missoula ("UltraBlend, SkyBlend, and Duramine"): https://www.roseburg.com/montana/montana-known-for-big-sky-gorgeous-views-and-high-quality-composite-panels/
- The current product catalog (https://www.roseburg.com/products/) lists only Engineered Wood, Exterior Trim, Lumber, MDF, Softwood Plywood and Wood Chips. The MDF page (https://www.roseburg.com/medium-density-fiberboard-mdf/) lists raw MDF grades only, with no TFL, Duramine or melamine mentions.
- The former Duramine page https://www.roseburg.com/thermally-fused-laminate-tfl/duramine/ now returns HTTP 404 (it redirects to the homepage in a browser). The last Wayback snapshot is 2024-03-30.
- Distributor brochures for Duramine are still online (e.g. paxtonwood.com, bluelinxco.com), but they predate the exit. They were not used as evidence of current availability.

## Pre-existing rows
The 24 rows (`ROS-MEL-*`, finish codes `ROS16WH`…) are placeholders identical to the other three manufacturers' rows. Even historically, Duramine finish codes were numeric (e.g. 751 Twilight Linea), not `ROS16WH`. They are deactivated rather than deleted; the reviewer may prefer to delete them.

## Unverified
A web-search summary claimed six new Duramine designs were shown at "IBS 2026" (lbmjournal.com, which returned 403). The article's ID sequence suggests it predates the 2024 exit. It could not be read, so it is not relied on. If Roseburg has relaunched TFL, re-run this research.
